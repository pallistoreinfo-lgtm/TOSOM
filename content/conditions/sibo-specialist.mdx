## Testing for SIBO

To determine whether a patient is dealing with SIBO or another condition like IBS, I use the Hydrogen Breath Test (HBT):

-   A non-invasive test that measures hydrogen and methane levels in the breath
-   Conducted after ingesting a specific sugar solution
