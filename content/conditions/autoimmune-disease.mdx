Research is increasingly showing a strong connection between autoimmune diseases and poor gut health. Just a few decades ago, cases of autoimmune disease were rare, today, over 50  
million Americans are affected. The Gut 911 Rx is a big step forward to eradicate this problem.

A poor diet is one of the most common but overlooked root causes of poor gut health. When we regularly consume “carbs from hell”—refined sugars, processed grains, and chemical-laden

because the damaged gut lining can’t properly absorb essential vitamins and minerals.

Combine this with the daily load of environmental toxins and it’s no wonder people are sick, tired, and inflamed. Healing begins when we nourish the gut with “carbs from heaven,” manage stress, and restore balance from the inside out.
