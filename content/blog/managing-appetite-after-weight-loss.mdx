### **Why Does Your Appetite Increase After Weight Loss?**

When you lose weight, your body goes into preservation gear. The body is programmed this way. Fearing starvation, the body ramps up production of ghrelin, the “[hunger hormone,” and reduces the production of leptin](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5357888/), the “I’m full” hormone.

But don’t think you are destined to make a bigger waistline. Instead, think of this phase as a time to reset your metabolism, re-train your brain, and feed your microbiome—your gut’s internal weight management system which plays a big role in controlling your appetite.

#### **1\. Eat More—of the Right Carbs** 

You read that right. Don’t starve yourself. In fact, that’s a guaranteed way to increase cravings. The key is choosing “carbs from heaven,” not “carbs from hell” (a distinction I explain in detail in my book \[Carbs from Heaven, Carbs from Hell\]). Stick with me to the end because I’m going to give you a free copy of the book, a $16.95 value on Amazon.

**Carbs from Heaven:**

-   Sweet potatoes
-   Lentils
-   Quinoa
-   Leafy greens
-   Berries
-   Steel-cut oats
-   All whole food carbs
-   Except gluten grains  

These foods nourish your body, support normal blood sugar, and fuel your hormones that keep you satisfied, not hungry—the body’s natural checks and balances to keep you satisfied.

**Carbs from Hell:**

-   White bread
-   Sugary cereals
-   Soda
-   Candy
-   Pastries
-   Ultra-processed snacks

These refined carbs spike your blood sugar and trigger your hunger signals making your appetite feel like a runaway train.

#### **2\. Start Your Day with Superfood Fuel (or Fasting)**

The first hour of your day sets the tone for the rest.

In my Supernatural Morning Routine, I recommend one of two strategies:

-   **Intermittent Fasting (IF)** – For those with stable blood sugar, the 16:8 fast (16 hours fast, 8-hour eating window) helps reset appetite signals and improves insulin sensitivity.
-   **Superfood Smoothie** – If fasting isn’t for you, a nutrient rich, low-glycemic smoothie featuring spirulina, chia seeds, and greens provides long lasting fuel without triggering food cravings.

For example spirulina has been shown to reduce appetite, lower inflammation, and balance blood sugar levels—important measures after weight loss.

#### **3\. Feed the Gut, Reduce Food Cravings**

The beneficial bacteria in your gut play a big role in controlling your appetite. An imbalance in your gut bacteria (your microbiome) can make you crave more sugar and refined carbs.

In my book Gut 911 Rx, I explain how healing your gut can significantly lower your food cravings.  Start with:

-   **Fermented foods:** sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, plain yogurt, etc.,
-   **Prebiotic fibers:** garlic, onions, leeks, asparagus, bananas
-   **Probiotic supplements:** strain-specific blends for post-weight loss and sugar cravings

A healthy gut can reduce food cravings dramatically, stimulate better mood, and provide more energy to stay on track.

#### **4\. Retrain Your Brain: Break the “Reward Hunger” Cycle**

Many people experience “unexplained hunger” after weight loss. Hunger that can occur shortly after a meal. It’s not physical—it’s emotional.

Ask yourself:

-   Am I bored?
-   Am I anxious?
-   Am I looking for a reward?

The solution isn’t always food. Try deep breathing, keeping a diet diary, or a 10 minute walk outdoors immediately following a meal. As I often say: “[You’re not hungry—you’re just not getting adequate nourishment.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7333005/)”

You may also benefit from positive thinking and visualization techniques. You can find these mindset tools in my Supernatural Morning book to create new mental habits and patterns. 

#### **5\. Don’t Fear Healthy Fats & Clean Protein Sources** 

Healthy fats and clean protein sources are essential to maintain a normal balance of nutrients.

During the day be sure to include:

-   Clean protein sources like beans and legumes, non-gluten whole grains, (wild caught fish like salmon, cod and other wild caught small fish-pasture raised chicken or turkey a couple times per week (optional).
-   Healthy fats (avocados, olive oil, raw nuts, seeds and cold water wild caught fish)
-   Fiber-rich fruits and veggies.

This trio sends a message to your brain: “I’m full, satisfied, and stable.”
