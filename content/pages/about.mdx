## About Dr. J

Dr. James D. Krystosik, D.C., directs The Other Side of Medicine. He has worked in chiropractic and functional medicine since 1986, with a focus on nutrition, digestive health, and root-cause evaluation.

He earned his Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Life University and completed undergraduate education in nutrition. He is also the author of five books on nutrition and natural medicine and hosts The Other Side of Medicine podcast.

## His approach

Dr. J begins by listening to the full health story, reviewing relevant information, and considering targeted testing when appropriate. Recommendations are personalized around the individual rather than based on a single symptom or a one-size-fits-all protocol.

His guiding principle is: **Don't guess—test.**
