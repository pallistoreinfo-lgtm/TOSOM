## Success Means More When You Have the Health to Enjoy It

You can build a career, grow a business, care for a family, and create financial security—but poor health can keep you from enjoying any of it. When digestion is unpredictable, energy disappears, inflammation limits movement, or chronic symptoms control your schedule, even ordinary pleasures become difficult.

That is why **wealth without health is worthless**. Health gives you the freedom to use your time, be present with the people you love, travel, serve others, stay independent, and enjoy the life you worked hard to create.

## Your Gut Is a Foundation, Not an Isolated System

The gut influences digestion, nutrient absorption, immune activity, inflammation, energy, mood, and metabolism. Treating isolated symptoms without examining their root causes can lead to temporary relief while the underlying imbalance remains.

Dr. James Krystosik’s approach begins with three practical principles:

1. **Common sense:** Give the body the nourishment, movement, rest, and mindset it needs.
2. **Science:** Use research and appropriate testing to replace guessing with useful information.
3. **Cooperation with nature:** Remove obstacles to healing and support the body’s built-in restorative capacity.
