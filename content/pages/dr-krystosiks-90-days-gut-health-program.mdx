After the Discovery Consultation, the next step on your journey can be the Gut Restoration Roadmap. It is a **life-changing health journey** designed to:

- Repair and support the lining of the gut
- Rebalance the microbiome—the beneficial bacteria living in the gut
- Restore digestive health
- Reduce inflammation
- Identify hidden food sensitivities and delayed food allergies
- Normalize bowel movements
- Improve energy, mood, and metabolism

The Roadmap combines **functional medicine, nutritional therapy, and proven clinical lifestyle strategies** to address the root causes of digestive illness—not merely the symptoms.

## How the Program Works

### 1. Personalized Assessment

You will begin with a simple [Gut Health Quiz](https://form.jotform.com/250364347478464). ### 2. The Gut Restoration Roadmap

You will receive a simple, actionable blueprint divided into three phases.

### Phase 1 — Detox & Reset

- Reduce inflammation
- Remove refined and highly processed foods while introducing whole foods and superfoods
- Reduce gas, bloating, and cramping
- Support the body's natural detoxification pathways
- Begin repairing the gut lining
- Identify hidden food allergies and intolerances
- Introduce targeted nutritional supplements when appropriate
- Begin stress-management and mindfulness practices

This phase is designed to calm the digestive system and clear the internal clutter so healing can begin.

### Phase 2 — Repair & Restore

- Support and repair the gut barrier
- Restore a healthy microbiome balance and address harmful microorganisms
- Support digestive enzymes and healthy stomach-acid function
- Improve nutrient absorption
- Reduce cravings through a gut-brain reset

Many people notice meaningful improvements during this phase, including better digestion, improved mood, more energy, and clearer thinking. Individual results vary.

### Phase 3 — Rebuild for Life

- Optimize daily habits
- Support the liver, pancreas, and gallbladder
- Build a gut-friendly lifestyle that lasts
- Develop sustainable, long-term eating patterns

This is where new habits become part of everyday life and help support long-term success.
